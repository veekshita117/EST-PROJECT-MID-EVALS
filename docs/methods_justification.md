# Methods Justification: Datasets, Metrics, and Models

This document provides a systematic, tabular justification for every dataset, metric, and statistical/machine learning model utilized across the research pipeline. Each choice is evaluated against rejected alternatives and accompanied by explicit methodological limitations.

---

## 1. Datasets

| Dataset | Choice | Why | Alternative Rejected | Limitation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **IUCN Expert Range Maps** | `rasterSp` 0.5° global raster grids across Mammalia, Amphibia, and Reptilia | Bypasses polygon intersection artifacts and geometry topological invalidities; eliminates human collector sampling effort bias; provides complete terrestrial coverage ($N=755$ at 5°, $N=4,395$ at 2°). | Raw vector shapefile clipping / buffering | Assumes homogeneous occupancy across entire geographic range extent, overestimating fine-scale local presence in fragmented habitats. |
| **Taxonomic Filtering** | Explicit exclusion of polar bear (*Ursus maritimus*) | Ecologically obligate marine mammal dependent on sea-ice substrate; lacks terrestrial macroclimatic niche fidelity. | Retention in terrestrial Mammalia | Retaining polar bears introduces spurious high-latitude terrestrial stability anomalies not representative of land assemblages. |
| **GBIF Point Occurrences** | 17,396 cleaned global point occurrences across 6 continents | Provides empirical observed record counts for historical baseline replication (Phase 6/Phase 9 econometric comparisons). | Unfiltered GBIF dump (>1B records) | Extreme spatial sampling bias: observer effort (`log_effort`) explains $>82-84\%$ of variance, confounding ecological signals. |
| **PaleoClim** | CHELSA-downscaled 2.5' bioclimatic surfaces across Present, Late Holocene, and LGM | High-resolution topographically downscaled climatology incorporating valley cold-air pooling and lapse rates. | Macro-GCM outputs without downscaling (e.g. raw 1°–2° PMIP3 grids) | PMIP3 ensemble carries structural model biases; precipitation anomalies in tropical montane zones retain simulation uncertainties. |
| **WorldClim** | CCSM4 paleoclimate surfaces across Present, Mid-Holocene, and LGM | Serves as independent paleoclimatic cross-validation product testing whether conclusions depend on climate reconstruction source. | Reliance on PaleoClim alone | Coarser baseline physical parameterization than CHELSA; differs in temporal boundary definitions (Mid-Holocene vs Late Holocene). |
| **Copernicus DEM** | GLO-90 global 90-meter digital elevation model streamed from AWS Open Data | High vertical accuracy ($\pm 4$ m) and global uniform coverage for calculating mean elevation and fine-scale relief heterogeneity. | SRTM 90m (void-filled) or ETOPO1 | SRTM truncates above 60°N; ETOPO1 is too coarse ($\sim 1.8$ km) to compute true topographic sheltering and micro-relief ranges. |

## 2. Quantitative Metrics

| Metric | Choice | Why | Alternative Rejected | Limitation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Biodiversity Target (Richness)** | $\ln(1 + \text{richness})$ | Normalizes highly right-skewed species count distributions; linearizes power-law species-area and species-energy relationships. | Raw species counts | Severe heteroscedasticity; heavy positive skew violates linear and Gaussian regression assumptions. |
| **Small-Range Richness** | Bottom quartile (Q25) of global range sizes | Directly tests whether historical climate refugia preferentially preserve narrow-ranged, low-dispersal endemic species. | Mean range size per cell | Mean range size is dominated by widespread cosmopolitan generalists; obscures localized refugial persistence. |
| **Weighted Endemism** | Sum of inverse range sizes ($\sum 1 / \text{range}_i$) | Standard macroecological metric assigning proportional credit to restricted-range species without arbitrary thresholding. | Unweighted range-size sum | Unweighted sums do not differentiate between cells containing widespread vs highly localized threatened taxa. |
| **Climate Velocity** | $\log_{10}(\text{temporal anomaly} / \text{spatial gradient})$ | Sandel et al. (2011) formulation quantifying the minimum speed species must migrate to track their thermal envelope. | Simple temperature anomaly ($\Delta T$) | Ignores topography: flat landscapes require massive geographical displacement under small temperature shifts. |
| **Velocity Gradient Floor** | Floor parameter $\epsilon = 10^{-4}$ °C/km | Prevents infinite velocities over completely flat terrain; scale sensitivity confirms rank stability ($\rho \ge 0.996$ across $10^{-5}$ to $10^{-3}$). | Zero floor or unconstrained division | Division by zero or near-zero produces extreme outlier spikes that distort regression leverage and correlation ranks. |
| **Refugial Area Fraction** | Overlap fraction of Present and LGM bioclimatic thermal envelopes | Explicit spatial indicator of continuous in situ climatic envelope persistence since the Last Glacial Maximum. | Binary presence/absence of LGM ice sheet | Fails to distinguish between non-glaciated arid deserts and stable, continuously buffered montane refugia. |
| **Sampling Effort Control** | Rarefaction ($n=20$) and $\ln(\text{n\_records})$ | Controls for global disparities in biological collection density across grid cells for GBIF models. | Uncorrected raw richness | Fails completely: cell richness in opportunistic data reflects human surveyor density rather than biological diversity. |
| **Spatial Autocorrelation** | Global Moran's $I$ on row-standardized $k$-NN weights ($k=5$ for 5°, $k=8$ for 2°) | Tests for residual spatial dependency and clustering on spherical surface; assesses if model innovations satisfy spatial independence. | Unadjusted Pearson/Spearman p-values | Naive p-values severely overestimate nominal significance due to inflated degrees of freedom under spatial autocorrelation. |
| **Confidence Intervals** | 20° $\times$ 20° cluster block bootstrap ($B = 1000$) | Preserves spatial autocorrelation structure within contiguous geographic blocks; yields honest empirical standard errors. | Standard cell-level bootstrap | Resampling individual cells breaks spatial clustering, producing artificially narrow confidence intervals. |
| **Multiple Testing Correction** | Benjamini-Hochberg FDR $q$-values using block-bootstrap $p$-values | Controls the False Discovery Rate across multiple correlation tiers and taxonomic groups using honest non-parametric $p$-values. | Bonferroni correction or naive FDR | Bonferroni is excessively conservative for correlated spatial tests; naive FDR ignores spatial dependence structure. |

## 3. Statistical & Machine Learning Models

| Model | Choice | Why | Alternative Rejected | Limitation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ordinary Least Squares (OLS)** | Parameter-free linear baseline with standardized predictors | Replicates Phase 6 and Phase 9 baseline econometric specifications; serves as transparent reference for parameter estimation. | Stepwise variable selection | Stepwise regression inflates Type I error rates, exaggerates coefficient sizes, and yields unstable feature subsets. |
| **Spatial Error Model (`spreg.ML_Error`)** | Maximum Likelihood spatial autoregressive error model: $u = \lambda W u + \varepsilon$ | Explicitly absorbs spatially correlated unobserved variables ($\lambda = 0.85 - 0.95, p < 10^{-5}$); renders residuals spatially uncorrelated. | Ignoring spatial dependence (plain OLS) | Standard errors are biased downward in OLS, inflating significance of macro-gradient predictors. |
| **Spatial Lag Model (`spreg.ML_Lag`)** | Maximum Likelihood spatial autoregressive lag model: $y = \rho W y + X\beta + \varepsilon$ | Tests for direct spatial contagion / spillover processes among neighboring grid cells. | Simultaneous Autoregressive (SAR) without lag comparison | AICs are only comparable within model families; Spatial Lag exhibits higher residual autocorrelation than Spatial Error. |
| **Quantile Regression** | Interior point quantile estimator across $\tau \in \{0.10, 0.50, 0.75, 0.90\}$ with block bootstrap | Directly evaluates whether climate velocity imposes an upper-bound ceiling constraint on maximum possible species richness. | Mean regression only | Mean regression fails to detect limiting-factor ceiling constraints where other unmeasured factors depress sub-maximal cells. |
| **Ridge Regression** | $L_2$-penalized regression with nested spatial `GridSearchCV` ($\alpha \in [10^{-3}, 10^{3}]$) | Shrinks collinear coefficients of contemporary climate and topography, preventing variance inflation and coefficient instability. | Lasso ($L_1$) regression | Lasso arbitrarily drops one of two highly correlated physical predictors (e.g., bio4 vs bio15) rather than sharing shrinkage. |
| **Random Forest Regressor** | 100 de-correlated trees with nested spatial hyperparameter tuning | Captures non-linear thresholds, environmental ceilings, and complex interactions without specifying parametric functions a priori. | Single decision tree or shallow CART | Single trees suffer from high variance, instability to spatial sampling shifts, and step-like discontinuities. |
| **HistGradientBoosting** | Histogram-binned gradient boosted trees with nested spatial tuning | Highly efficient fitting of non-linear step responses and monotonic functions across 4,395 cells with native handling of tabular features. | Deep neural networks (MLPs) | Neural networks require extensive hyperparameter tuning, lack spatial inductive bias on tabular grids, and risk overfitting. |
| **Spatial Cross-Validation** | 20° $\times$ 20° spatial-block GroupKFold ($k=5$) with 3-fold nested inner tuning | Guarantees zero geographic overlap between training and testing folds; measures true out-of-fold spatial predictive transfer skill. | Random $k$-fold cross-validation | Random $k$-fold leaks spatial autocorrelation from adjacent training cells into test folds, producing spuriously high $R^2$. |
| **LOCO Generalization** | Leave-One-Continent-Out cross-validation across 6 continents | Rigorously tests whether macro-evolutionary climate relationships calibrated in one realm transfer across distinct biogeographic histories. | In-sample goodness-of-fit ($R^2$) | In-sample $R^2$ cannot test macroecological transferability and masks intercontinental calibration collapses. |

## 4. Advanced Methodological Extensions

| Method / Model | Choice | Why | Alternative Rejected | Limitation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Spatial Eigenvector Mapping (MEM)** | Doubly-centered $k=8$ haversine spatial weight matrix; positive Moran eigenvectors ($I > 0.1$); fold-safe out-of-sample projection | Absorbs spatial autocorrelation directly into feature space; reduces residual Moran's $I$ by 31–44% in HGB; prevents optimistic spatial leakage across CV folds. | Standard coordinates $(x,y)$ or full sample eigenvector decomposition without fold-safe projection | Using $(x,y)$ assumes linear spatial trend; extracting eigenvectors on all data leaks test geometry into training features. |
| **Orthogonalized Climate Stability** | Residualization of stability against absolute latitude, elevation, and relief using Ridge ($\alpha=10^{-3}$) fitted on training folds only | Isolates the unique incremental explanatory power of palaeoclimate stability purged of geographical and topographic collinearity; drops VIF to 1.00. | Evaluating raw stability alone or simple unpenalized OLS residualization | Raw stability confluates true historical persistence with modern latitudinal and elevational gradients; unpenalized OLS can overfit on small folds. |
| **Buffered Spatial Cross-Validation** | 5 spatial folds with a strict 1000 km haversine exclusion buffer around test observations | Completely eliminates boundary spatial proximity leakage where adjacent cells across block borders share autocorrelated climate/biodiversity noise. | Unbuffered block CV or random CV | Discards 15–21% of candidate training cells located within the buffer, reducing effective training sample size. |
| **Stochastic Frontier Analysis (SFA)** | Normal-Half-Normal Maximum Likelihood production ceiling model: $y = X\beta + v - u, u \ge 0$ | Directly models the macroecological limiting-factor ceiling hypothesis, confirming significant negative residual skewness ($p < 10^{-4}$) and testing velocity as an upper bound. | Quantile regression alone or OLS | Quantile regression does not separate idiosyncratic observation noise ($v$) from structural environmental inefficiency ($u$). |
| **Joint Multi-Taxa Modelling** | Unified Hierarchical MixedLM with cell random intercepts and Seemingly Unrelated Regressions across Reptilia, Mammalia, and Amphibia | Formally tests whether taxon-specific sensitivity contrasts hold simultaneously while accounting for shared cell-level unobserved drivers and sampling effort. | Fitting independent single-taxon regressions in isolation | Separate regressions fail to account for inter-taxa residual correlation and cannot conduct formal joint Wald hypothesis tests on slope equality. |
